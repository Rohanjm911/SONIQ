### SONIQ — Pure Sound. Infinite Possibilities.

SONIQ is a next-generation, cross-platform desktop music player designed to bring together audiophile-grade audio playback, extensive format compatibility, intelligent music discovery, and a premium listening experience.

Built with C++20, Qt 6, and FFmpeg, SONIQ aims to deliver a powerful yet intuitive music player for Windows, Linux, and macOS. From managing extensive local music libraries to exploring advanced audio controls, SONIQ is designed for listeners who value both sound quality and thoughtful design.

**Key Highlights**

* 🎵 Extensive audio format support.
* 🎧 Audiophile-focused playback with bit-perfect output where supported and verifiable.
* 🎚️ Advanced equalizers and configurable audio processing.
* 📚 Smart music library management and playlist organization.
* 🎤 Online lyrics, album artwork, and metadata integration.
* ✨ Premium UI with smooth animations, dark and light themes, and an immersive Now Playing experience.
* 🔒 Offline-first architecture with a focus on privacy.

**Tech Stack:** C++20 · Qt 6 · QML · FFmpeg · SQLite · TagLib · CMake

SONIQ combines powerful audio engineering with modern desktop design to create a listening experience that feels as good as it sounds.
